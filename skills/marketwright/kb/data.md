# Data and current practice

Numbers with their sources (table at the end). Medians of other games, never a forecast. Re-open the source before quoting a figure in anything published.

## Wishlists

- Wishlists are worth less than they were: first-month sales per wishlist fell from 0.45 (34 games, 2020-22) to 0.22 (126 games, 2023-25); six months 0.82 to 0.43; one year 0.99 to 0.70. They still predict sales about as well (R squared 0.77, then 0.72). Steam releases rose from about 14,000 (2023) to nearly 21,000 (2025). [D1]
- First-week sales: median 10.5 percent of launch wishlists for Aug-Oct 2024 releases with 5,000+ [D7]; 0.15x for 2024-25 games with 25,000+ [D1]. The old 20 to 30 percent rules of thumb are too optimistic.
- Popular Upcoming: the bar rose from about 7,000 wishlists to about 100,000 in June 2026 (developer estimates; Valve publishes none). A small game should not plan around it. [D3]
- Old wishlists do not go stale in a way that matters (one game's monthly cohorts averaged 8.02 percent conversion; the Next Fest cohort 5.51). One example only. [D4]

## Next Fest

- February 2026 fest, 182 responses, median wishlists gained during the fest by wishlists before it: under 1,000 gained 322; 1,000 to 9,999 gained 1,006; 10,000 to 99,999 gained 5,215; 100,000+ gained 12,882. The fest multiplies the audience you bring. [D5]
- Most demo players will not wishlist, and that is normal (Zukowski, June 2026). [D3]
- Talk: "Marketing Your Game Under the New Steam Rules", Zukowski, GDC 2025 Independent Games Summit. [D6]

## Trailers

- Gameplay in the first seconds; viewers do not wait 15 to 30 seconds, and many scrub the bar for raw gameplay. Stacking many trailers works against you. The lead trailer should be all gameplay (Derek Lieu). [D8]
- Store trailers autoplay muted: the first seconds must read without sound. No evidence-backed ideal length; do not open on a logo (UNVERIFIED wording).

## Press kits

- presskit() (dopresskit) is an old PHP install with no recent updates (status UNVERIFIED). Current routes: presskit.html (free, MIT, static), IMPRESS Press Kitty (hosted), presskit.gg (vendor: free for one game, paid from $69 a year), or a page made with sitewright. [D9]
- Contents: factsheet (name, developer, date, platforms, price, links), short and long description, features, trailer link, full-size screenshots and GIFs, logo and key art with transparent versions, team and contact, quotes and awards, streaming and monetisation terms, plain statements of AI use and Early Access status.

## Creator outreach tools

- Keymailer: free tier, paid from about $24.99 a month per game (third-party, check keymailer.co). [D10]
- Lurkit: custom pricing, 15 percent commission on Quests (UNVERIFIED).
- Woovit: on 2026-11-02 woovit.com served an unrelated betting page; do not recommend it until its status is confirmed.
- Steam Curator Connect (kb/steam.md) is free.

## Socials and devlogs

- No rigorous study ranks TikTok, Shorts, Reddit, Bluesky or X by wishlists. Steam's own events drive most wishlists. Treat platform claims as anecdotes and say so.

## Launch timing

- The weekday matters less than indies think (Zukowski, 2023). Avoid major Steam sales and big launches in your genre; lock the date six to eight weeks out (consensus, no dataset). `MKW timeline` warns on sale clashes.

## Roblox discovery [D11]

- Recommended for You ranks by play-through rate, first-play bounce (negative), play days and playtime per user; then co-play, qualified sessions, spend days, Robux per user. Only users who arrived from Recommended for You count.
- Playtime counts up to 60 minutes per user per game per day. Users first acquired from ads are not counted for ranking. Copies of existing games are not prioritised.
- So: fix the first session (bounce) before buying ads; ads buy consideration, not rank.
