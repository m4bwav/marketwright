# Wishlists and current indie marketing practice

Research for marketwright. Checked 2026-11-02 unless a line says otherwise.
Rule: every number here was read on the page cited. Things not read on a primary or named page are marked UNVERIFIED.
Some figures came through a fetch tool that summarises pages. Re-open the page before quoting a number in shipped text.

## 1. Chris Zukowski (howtomarketagame.com)

### Wishlist value is falling (newest and most important)
- Zukowski's October 2026 post compared 34 games from 2020-2022 with 126 from 2023-2025. It used Steamworks reports that developers sent him. First-month sales per wishlist fell from 0.45 to 0.22. Six-month sales fell from 0.82 to 0.43. One-year sales fell from 0.99 to 0.70. [S1]
- Wishlists still predict sales about as well as before. R squared of launch-day wishlists against first-month sales was 0.77, then 0.72. Wishlist velocity alone gave 0.70 for 2023-2025. [S1]
- SteamDB counts cited there: about 14,000 new releases in 2023 and nearly 21,000 in 2025. [S1]
- Read through Inven Global's write-up. The original Zukowski post (dated October 13, 2026) was not opened. UNVERIFIED wording.

### Popular Upcoming threshold changed in June 2026
- The old rule of thumb was 7,000 to 10,000 wishlists before launch. [S2]
- His June 25, 2026 post "How the Steam Personal Calendar affects your launch" says Valve raised the bar for Popular Upcoming from about 7,000 wishlists to about 100,000. It quotes Valve: "The contents of this tab have been updated in response to player feedback." [S3]
- Valve publishes no threshold. These are estimates from developer data.
- Advice to give: a small game should no longer plan around Popular Upcoming.

### Cohort conversion (one game, Song of Iron)
- "Do wishlists get old" (Jan 27, 2025): average monthly cohort conversion 8.02%, standard deviation about 2%. Next Fest cohort converted lowest at 5.51%. A Popular Upcoming month converted at 9.13%. Cosmoteer's average cohort converted at 19.31%. [S4]
- His conclusion: old wishlists do not go stale in a way that matters. One example only.

### Next Fest benchmarks
- Survey of the February 2026 Next Fest, 182 responses. Median wishlists gained during the fest, by wishlists before it: 0-999 gave 322 (n=71). 1,000-9,999 gave 1,006 (n=60). 10,000-99,999 gave 5,215 (n=36). 100,000+ gave 12,882 (n=7). Page updated April 8, 2026. [S5]
- Lesson: Next Fest multiplies what you bring. It does not rescue a game with no audience.
- June 30, 2026 post title: "Most people will play your demo and not wishlist it and that is ok". [S3]

## 2. Other data sources

### GameDiscoverCo (Simon Carless)
- Aug-Oct 2024 releases with at least 5,000 launch wishlists: median first-week sales were 10.5% of wishlists. "Lower than GameDiscoverCo expected." Reported by Game World Observer, Evgeny Obedkov, 2024-12-06. [S7]
- Games released 2024-2025 with at least 25,000 wishlists: median first-week sales 0.15x launch wishlists. A 2024 survey of games with over 10,000 wishlists: median 0.17x. As cited in [S1].
- Older rules of thumb, as cited in [S1]: Mike Rose (No More Robots, GDC 2020) put first-week sales at 20-30% of wishlists. Carless gave a median of 15-25% depending on size. Both are now too optimistic.
- Takeaway: use 10-20% first-week conversion as a range, lower for games above $10. Never promise a number.

## 3. Trailers
- Simon Carless, GameDiscoverCo, 2022-09-19: on Steam viewers need gameplay fast. "15-30 seconds in? They're not going to wait." Trailers autoplay on Steam and console store pages. Many viewers scrub the progress bar for raw gameplay (citing Zukowski's research). Stacking many trailers is "tactically unsound". [S8]
- Derek Lieu, quoted in the same piece: the lead Steam trailer should be all gameplay, with gameplay in the first seconds. A second, deeper trailer is optional. [S8]
- Lieu's blog warns against opening on a logo. Seen in a search summary only. UNVERIFIED wording. Lieu's site: https://www.derek-lieu.com (not opened this pass).
- Length: no evidence-backed ideal. Secondary sites say 45-90 or 60-90 seconds. Lieu reportedly says not to fixate on length. UNVERIFIED.
- Muted autoplay matters: make the first seconds readable with no sound. [S8]
- Vertical short form: an Outlook Respawn opinion piece says TikTok organic reach collapsed in early 2025. No data behind it. UNVERIFIED.

## 4. Press kits
- presskit() (dopresskit, Rami Ismail) is a PHP install. Vendors of alternatives say it has not been updated since about 2014-2017 and breaks on modern hosting. No official discontinuation statement found. UNVERIFIED status. [S9]
- Alternatives:
  - presskit.html by Pixelnest: free, MIT, static site re-implementation. https://github.com/pixelnest/presskit.html
  - IMPRESS (impress.games) Press Kitty: hosted. https://impress.games/dopresskit-alternative
  - presskit.gg: WordPress plugin, free tier for 1 game, paid from $69 a year per its own docs. Vendor claim. [S9]
  - PressKitHero: hosted, $20-80 a month per a presskit.gg comparison. Competitor claim, UNVERIFIED.
- Contents (common practice, no single study): factsheet (name, developer, release date, platforms, price, links), short and long description, feature list, trailer link, screenshots and GIFs at full size, logo and key art with transparent versions, team and contact, press quotes and awards, terms for streaming and monetisation. Honest marketing note: state AI use and early access status plainly.

## 7. Launch timing
- Zukowski (2023-02-14): indies spend far too long picking a launch day and it matters less than they think. https://howtomarketagame.com/2023/02/14/whats-the-best-day-to-release-an-indie-game/ (seen via search summary)
- SteamData.ai (April 2026, vendor blog): about 40% of releases land on Thursday. Never launch during a major Steam sale. UNVERIFIED method.
- Steam Page Analyzer argues for Tuesday, with no dataset. UNVERIFIED.
- Consistent advice across sources: avoid major Steam sales and AAA launches in your genre. Lock the date 6-8 weeks out.
- New in June 2026: the Steam Personal Calendar affects launches, per Zukowski's June 25, 2026 post. Detail not read. [S3]
- Launching right after Next Fest: not researched this pass. UNVERIFIED.

## 8. Roblox discovery
- Roblox docs, "Discovery" (no update date shown, (c)2026): Recommended for You uses retrieval then ranking. [S11]
- Top signals: play-through rate, first-play bounce rate (negative), play days per user, playtime per user. Next: co-play days, qualified sessions, spend days, Robux per user. Signals count only users who joined from Recommended for You. [S11]
- Playtime is capped at 60 minutes per user, per game, per day. [S11]
- Ads can speed up organic consideration, but "Roblox doesn't count the engagement, monetization, or retention of users first acquired from ads" in ranking. [S11]
- Signals are per-user averages, so small games with engaged players are not penalised. Experiences that copy existing ones are not prioritised. Seen in search summary of the same docs.
- Honest advice: fix the first session (bounce rate) before buying ads.
