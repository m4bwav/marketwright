# Steam: Valve's rules and numbers

Every row was read on the Steamworks page named in brackets, checked 2026-11-02 (source list at the end). Re-check a row older than 60 days before a plan depends on it. UNVERIFIED means not found on a primary page.

## Graphical assets [S1, S2, S3]

| Asset | Pixels | Required |
|---|---|---|
| Header capsule | 920 x 430 | yes |
| Small capsule | 462 x 174 (logo nearly fills it) | yes |
| Main capsule | 1232 x 706 | yes |
| Vertical capsule | 748 x 896 (seasonal sale pages) | yes |
| Page background | 1438 x 810 | no (built from the last screenshot) |
| Library capsule | 600 x 900 (logo, subtitle, no other text) | yes |
| Library hero | 3840 x 1240 .png, art only | yes |
| Library logo | 1280 wide and/or 720 tall .png, transparent | yes |
| Event cover / header | 800 x 450 / 1920 x 622 | for events |

- These sizes started in August 2024; old sizes are no longer accepted.
- Screenshots: at least 5, at least 1920 x 1080, 16:9, gameplay only (no concept art, renders, awards or marketing copy). At least four marked suitable for all ages.
- Capsule text (rules since 2022-09-01): only the art, the game name and an official subtitle. No review scores, awards, discount text, calls to action ("Wishlist now"), or other products. Temporary text goes in an Artwork Override, up to one month, localised, describing content already out. Breaking this can cost visibility and sale eligibility.

## Trailers [S4]

- .mp4, .mov or .wmv; H.264 and AAC preferred; up to 1920 x 1080 at 30 or 60 fps, 5,000+ Kbps.
- The first two trailers show before the screenshots. Steam cuts a 6-second micro trailer from the first one; it cannot be customised. Store trailers autoplay muted.
- Valve "highly recommends" the first trailer be mostly gameplay.

## Text, tags, Coming Soon, release [S5 to S8]

- Short description: "a few hundred characters" (exact limit UNVERIFIED), plain text, no time-bound phrases ("Now Available").
- No links, written domains or QR codes in the description; no fake Steam buttons or prices; no promoting other games. Text in images must be translated per language, so textless images localise cheaply.
- Tags: at least 5 before launch, up to 20; only the top 20 count and some filters weigh the first 15. Order sets starting weight; players' tags shift it later.
- Coming Soon page live at least two weeks before release. Submit the page for review at least 7 business days before you want it live; review typically takes 3 to 5 business days. The build is reviewed too. Release does not happen by itself: you press Release App.

## Next Fest and themed fests [S9, S10]

- One Next Fest per game, ever. Unreleased through the fest's end, public store page, public demo by the start.
- Demo review: 5 weeks before the fest to be in the Press Preview, 3 weeks for the fest. Press Preview starts 11 days before.
- Three a year (February, June, October). Published: 2027-02-22 to 03-01 and 2027-06-14 to 21. February 2027 (checked 2026-11-03 on partner.steamgames.com/doc/marketing/upcoming_events/nextfest/feb_2027): registration closes Jan 10 11:59pm PST; demo build and store page in review by Jan 18 for the Press Preview (Feb 11); everything else by Feb 1. June 2027 deadlines not yet published.
- Themed fests have their own opt-in; discounted games are prioritised, Coming Soon games and demos welcome. Listed: Auto-Battler RPG Nov 16-23 2026; Desktop Companion Jan 14-18; Shop Keeper Jan 25-Feb 1; Sheep Feb 4-8; Couch Co-Op Feb 8-15; Rhythm Mar 8-15; Dinos vs Robots Mar 29-Apr 5; Racing Apr 12-19; Witch Apr 22-26; Fighting Apr 26-May 3; RTS May 10-17; Mountaineering May 31-Jun 3 (2027).
- Seasonal sales: Winter Dec 17 2026-Jan 4 2027; Spring Mar 18-25 2027; Summer Jun 24-Jul 8 2027. A game must be released 30 days before a sale starts to join.

## Visibility [S11, S12]

- Wishlisters are emailed at launch, at leaving Early Access, and on discounts of 20 percent or more.
- Valve: wishlists are not a factor in algorithmic visibility, with exceptions like Popular Upcoming.
- Update Visibility Rounds exist for major updates after launch visibility ends.
- A demo can trigger one wishlist notification within 14 days of its release; notifications have a two-week cooldown per app.
- Discovery Queue and Upcoming list mechanics: UNVERIFIED.

## Discounts [S13]

- Launch discount 10 to 40 percent for 7 to 14 days, set before release (docs suggest 10 to 15).
- Other discounts 10 to 95 percent, 1 to 14 days, 30 days apart (since 2023-01-01). Seasonal sale discounts are exempt from the cooldown.

## AI content disclosure [S14]

- Content survey: Pre-Generated (made with AI during development and consumed by players: art, sound, text, localisation) and Live-Generated (made while the game runs; describe your guardrails). Tools used only for efficiency are "not the focus". Press reports date this narrowing to January 2026 (UNVERIFIED on Valve's page). Disclosure is required since January 2024.

## Reviews, keys, curators [S15 to S17]

- No reviews in exchange for games, DLC, money or other rewards; press and creator copies are the exception. Never ask for reviews from inside the game. No multiple accounts or coercion.
- Only Steam purchases count toward the score; key activations do not. Free copies should be disclosed by the reviewer.
- Keys: up to 5,000 by default; release-override keys about 2,500, for betas and press, never sold. A free key giveaway needs the same deal on Steam unless under 100 keys. Fraudsters pose as journalists: treat keys like cash.
- Curator Connect: up to 100 curators, 5 copies each, works before release, cannot be revoked once accepted.

## Playtests and demos [S12, S18]

- Playtest: free, no store page of its own, signups on the base page, no reviews of the main game, wishlists untouched.
- Demo: a separate Demo app linked to the base game; an optional demo page needs a trailer and 5 demo screenshots, and only it carries demo reviews.
