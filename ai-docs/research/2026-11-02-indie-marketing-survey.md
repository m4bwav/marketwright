## Headlines

1. **Wishlists are worth about half what they were.** First-month sales per wishlist fell from 0.45 (2020-22) to 0.22 (2023-25) in Zukowski's data; first-week medians are 10 to 15 percent. Old 20 to 30 percent rules are wrong now.
2. **Popular Upcoming is out of reach for small games** since June 2026 (about 100,000 wishlists, developer estimates).
3. **Next Fest multiplies what you bring**: median gain 322 for games under 1,000 wishlists, 1,006 for 1,000 to 9,999 (February 2026 survey).
4. **Valve's rules are strict and checkable**: capsule text limited to name and subtitle since 2022, sizes changed August 2024, Next Fest once per game with 5- and 3-week demo deadlines, AI disclosure for content players consume.
5. **Law caught up with fake reviews**: FTC 16 CFR 465 (2024-10-21), UK DMCC (2025-04-06), updated ASA/CAP influencer guidance (October 2026).
6. **No existing tool covers honest indie marketing with community rules.** General marketing skill packs and Steam or Reddit MCP servers exist; Reddit MCP servers need API access, so the plugin does not depend on them.
7. **Reddit is readable only through RSS.** Fetches of reddit.com fail; everscout's RSS probe confirmed 29 genre and indie subreddits alive and 2 dead on 2026-11-03. Rules pages stay unread live.
